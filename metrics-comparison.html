The 4% critical tier is where the reversal came from — complaints, press coverage, and regulatory attention concentrated almost entirely here. It's invisible in the blended average, but it drives the majority of reputational and regulatory risk.

How the average hid it

Blended CSAT = (86% × 94) + (10% × 61) + (4% × 22)  =  84.0%
Blended NPS = (86% × 61) + (10% × 8) + (4% × −38)  =  +42.0

Because the critical tier is only 4% of volume, its collapse barely moves the company-wide number — even though it's the tier carrying the highest financial exposure, the most vulnerable customers, and nearly all of the regulatory and reputational risk. This is the exact mechanism that let the original failure go undetected until it became public.
